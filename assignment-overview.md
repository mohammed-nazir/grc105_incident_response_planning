# Assignment Overview

## Scenario
Northbridge Skills Institute faces a suspected ransomware event involving FIN-PC07, FS01, LMS availability, unusual identity activity, backup access, and a suspicious 1.2 GB outbound transfer.

## Declared severity
**Critical**, based on material file-service disruption, examination impact, suspicious privileged access to backup infrastructure, and a credible but unconfirmed confidentiality risk.

## Core deliverables
- Evidence-linked incident assessment
- CSIRT structure and RACI
- Ordered first-hour and 24-hour playbook
- Evidence preservation and chain-of-custody approach
- Communications and escalation plan
- Tabletop exercise and measurable remediation plan
- Case-study reflection
